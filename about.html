Who we are

A thoughtful approach
to global travel retail.

Vantage GTR, an international travel retail distribution company headquartered in the United Arab Emirates, focused on connecting brands, products and retail opportunities across the global travel retail industry.

We specialize in sourcing, stocking, and distributing a diverse range of products, including perfumes and fragrances, cosmetics and beauty, consumer electronics, travel accessories, and lifestyle essentials. Our multi-category approach enables us to serve the evolving needs of travel retail operators while creating new opportunities for brands seeking to expand their market presence.

We believe that successful distribution is about more than supplying products. It is about building trusted relationships, understanding market needs, maintaining product availability, and creating sustainable value for every partner we work with.

Who We Are

Vantage GTR was established with a clear ambition: to build a reliable, commercially focused distribution business serving the dynamic global travel retail market.

We work to bridge the gap between brand owners and travel retail operators by combining product sourcing, inventory management, direct distribution, and market development. Our approach is centered on understanding each market, identifying the right opportunities, and delivering solutions that support long-term business growth.

With a broadening product portfolio and a partnership-driven mindset, we aim to help brands reach new customers while enabling retailers to access relevant products through a dependable distribution partner.
